🔒 Privacy-First AI · 🚧 Actively Refining

Your AI, Your Machine, Your Data

LocalMind is a sleek web-based chat interface that connects to locally-hosted LLM models via FastAPI and llama-cpp-python. No cloud, no API keys, no data leaving your machine.

Built for anyone who wants the power of ChatGPT-like conversations without the privacy trade-offs. Supports GGUF models (Gemma, Mistral, LLaMA), streaming responses, markdown rendering, and code highlighting — all running on your hardware.

This project is under active refinement — cross-platform support, hardware-adaptive tuning, config-driven setup, and security hardening are all in progress.

✦ It won't outthink ChatGPT or out-charm Claude — but it'll never whisper your secrets to a server. 🤫
